# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Keep discussions technical and actionable.
- Assume maintainers and contributors are trying to improve the project.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Publishing private information without consent
- Threats or harassment
- Sustained off-topic disruption
- Abusive or discriminatory comments

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may lead to being blocked from participating.
